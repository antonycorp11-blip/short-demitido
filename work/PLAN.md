# "Fui demitido pelos meus personagens" — shot plan

1080x1920, 30 fps, 1395 frames (46.5 s). Voice is Portuguese. Exact sentence/word frame times: `work/timeline.json`
(sentence spans + key-word frames). Every visual reaction lands 0-3 frames BEFORE its key word.

## Cast (bean-head cards, roman.js style — add outfits to OUTFITS)
- **CRIADOR** (the narrator/game dev): purple hoodie, messy dark hair, headphones around neck, jeans. Faces: smile, shock, panic, jaw-drop.
- **MINEIROS** (3 of them, slightly different colours): orange overalls, yellow hard hat with a head lamp, dirty cheeks, one with a beard. Prop: pickaxe.
- **RH** (HR rep): grey suit, red tie, glasses, clipboard, neat side-part hair.
- In shot 9-10 the CRIADOR swaps to a hard hat + pickaxe (detached-part card or outfit switch).

## World
Two zones in one continuous 3D set:
- **Dev desk** (left/front): wooden desk, chair, keyboard, mug, and a GIANT floating monitor (dark frame, glowing screen plane) that shows diegetic UI panels.
- **The mine** (behind the monitor, revealed in shot 2): a hill with a mine entrance (wooden beam frame, dark tunnel), rails, a minecart with ore, ore rocks with gold/blue crystals, lanterns on posts, pine trees, mountains, painted clouds.
- **RH booth** pops up in shot 4: small plaster kiosk with a sign "RH".
- Diegetic text (must read correctly, not mirrored), all Portuguese:
  monitor prompt "Cria um jogo simples de mineração", picket signs "GREVE!", "FÉRIAS JÁ!", "RH" booth sign,
  monitor panels "ACABA COM ESSA GREVE AGORA!", "✔ Problema resolvido.", red notification "Você foi demitido.",
  second notification "Seu primeiro turno começa em 5 minutos.", and a sign "MINA" over the tunnel.
  No subtitles/captions.
